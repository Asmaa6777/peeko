Why Peeko exists

So that's Peeko. You keep doing the work, and it does the ticking. Here's how.

How a box gets ticked

You do the thing. Peeko checks. It ticks itself.

  1. 1

    You do the thing

    Read a paper in Preview for half an hour. You don't open Peeko, and you don't tick anything.

  2. 2

    Peeko looks at the evidence

    Every 10 seconds it notes which app is in front and whether you've touched the keyboard. That adds up to 30 active minutes in Preview.

    Preview · active · 30:00
  3. 3

    It checks itself off

    The task goes green, Peeko beams, and you get one quiet note.

Why only checkable things

A list you tick yourself is easy to lie to. Even by accident.

If it can't be checked, it's not here

"Drink water" sounds nice, but no Mac can see it. Peeko's Explore page only has things it can actually check, so a green tick means something happened.

Evidence, not policing

The signals are evidence, not a lie detector. Leave a PDF open and nudge the mouse, and it'll count as reading. That's fine. Miss something, and Peeko just says "no worries".

Room for your own

Some things only you know. You can add a few tasks of your own and tap them, but they live apart from the list your Mac checks.

Three faces

You can tell how your day is going from the eyes.

Every task wears one of Peeko's faces, and the menu bar icon shows the face for the whole day.

Privacy

Checked on your Mac. Kept on your Mac.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. No server, no analytics, no telemetry.

Plain files

Your tasks and history are JSON in ~/Library/Application Support/Peeko/. Read them, back them up, or delete them.

Only what you turn on

The online checks (GitHub, your feed, Duolingo) read public pages, and only if you switch them on. Your activity, notes and repos are checked on your Mac and never leave it.

Install

Paste one line into Terminal.

curl -fsSL https://peeko.stack42.tech/install.sh | bash

It downloads the latest release, checks its checksum, puts Peeko in Applications, and opens it. Run it again to update. Read the script first if you like; it's short.

Or download the latest DMG

Prefer the DMG?

Download the latest DMG (or pick a version from Releases) and drag Peeko into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time:

  1. Open Peeko once and close the warning.
  2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Click Open Anyway next to Peeko.

Build it yourself

All you need is Apple's Command Line Tools. No Xcode.

xcode-select --install
git clone https://github.com/STACK-42/peeko.git
cd peeko
make install

On Windows?

An early preview for Windows 10 and 11. Download the zip (v1), unzip it somewhere you'll keep it, and open Peeko.exe. It isn't signed yet, so the first time:

  1. Click More info on the "Windows protected your PC" box.
  2. Click Run anyway.
  3. Look for Peeko's face in the tray (it may be under the ^ arrow).

Everything works except the Time Machine and Reminders tasks, which are Mac only.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later on an Apple silicon Mac, or Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) for the preview.
